# 11 Intervals for auxiliary equipment

This section collects the intervals for pumps, thermostats and power supplies. It neither replaces nor repeats the intervals for the measuring instrument, which are in section 2.

## How often

Verification of auxiliary equipment is carried out every twenty-four months. A thermostat is calibrated every six months and after any repair of its heater.

## Who may carry it out

A service engineer carries out checks on auxiliary equipment without separate accreditation. An accredited laboratory is not required for this work.
