# 33 Service card for the КЛ-960

This card covers the КЛ-960 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The КЛ-960 is a laboratory calibrator. The card applies to every build of the КЛ-960 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The КЛ-960 is verified every 12 months. The КЛ-960 is calibrated every 1 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the КЛ-960 is the reference cell. It is replaced every 24 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the КЛ-960 requires an engineer authorised to level 4. An authorisation below level 4 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The КЛ-960 is stored between 18 and 24 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the КЛ-960 is due within 10 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
