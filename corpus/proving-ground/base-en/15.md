# 15 Commissioning report

A commissioning report is written once, when the instrument is brought into service. The report for a routine visit is described in section 10 and has a different content.

## What the report contains

A commissioning report carries the serial number, the date of entry into service, the list of settings made, and the signatures of both parties.

## When it is due

A commissioning report is due within ten working days of the date the instrument entered service.
