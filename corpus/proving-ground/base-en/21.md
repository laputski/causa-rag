# 21 Service card for the ХР-350

This card covers the ХР-350 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The ХР-350 is a laboratory chromatograph. The card applies to every build of the ХР-350 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The ХР-350 is verified every 12 months. The ХР-350 is calibrated every 2 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the ХР-350 is the separation column. It is replaced every 18 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the ХР-350 requires an engineer authorised to level 4. An authorisation below level 4 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The ХР-350 is stored between 15 and 28 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the ХР-350 is due within 10 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
