# 37 Service card for the ЦФ-180

This card covers the ЦФ-180 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The ЦФ-180 is a laboratory centrifuge. The card applies to every build of the ЦФ-180 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The ЦФ-180 is verified every 12 months. The ЦФ-180 is calibrated every 3 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the ЦФ-180 is the lid gasket. It is replaced every 9 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the ЦФ-180 requires an engineer authorised to level 3. An authorisation below level 3 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The ЦФ-180 is stored between 5 and 35 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the ЦФ-180 is due within 5 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
