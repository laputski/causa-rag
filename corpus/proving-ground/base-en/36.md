# 36 Service card for the ЦФ-130

This card covers the ЦФ-130 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The ЦФ-130 is a laboratory centrifuge. The card applies to every build of the ЦФ-130 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The ЦФ-130 is verified every 12 months. The ЦФ-130 is calibrated every 6 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the ЦФ-130 is the lid gasket. It is replaced every 12 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the ЦФ-130 requires an engineer authorised to level 3. An authorisation below level 3 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The ЦФ-130 is stored between 5 and 35 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the ЦФ-130 is due within 5 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
