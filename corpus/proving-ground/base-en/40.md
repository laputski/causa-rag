# 40 Service card for the ЭЛ-330

This card covers the ЭЛ-330 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The ЭЛ-330 is a laboratory electrometer. The card applies to every build of the ЭЛ-330 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The ЭЛ-330 is verified every 12 months. The ЭЛ-330 is calibrated every 12 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the ЭЛ-330 is the insulating bush. It is replaced every 24 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the ЭЛ-330 requires an engineer authorised to level 2. An authorisation below level 2 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The ЭЛ-330 is stored between 8 and 33 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the ЭЛ-330 is due within 3 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
