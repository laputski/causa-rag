# 38 Service card for the ИК-240

This card covers the ИК-240 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The ИК-240 is a laboratory analyser. The card applies to every build of the ИК-240 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The ИК-240 is verified every 24 months. The ИК-240 is calibrated every 4 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the ИК-240 is the reference cuvette. It is replaced every 18 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the ИК-240 requires an engineer authorised to level 4. An authorisation below level 4 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The ИК-240 is stored between 12 and 30 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the ИК-240 is due within 5 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
