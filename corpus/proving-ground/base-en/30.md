# 30 Service card for the ПР-810

This card covers the ПР-810 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The ПР-810 is a laboratory sampler. The card applies to every build of the ПР-810 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The ПР-810 is verified every 36 months. The ПР-810 is calibrated every 12 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the ПР-810 is the dosing needle. It is replaced every 6 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the ПР-810 requires an engineer authorised to level 2. An authorisation below level 2 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The ПР-810 is stored between 5 and 38 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the ПР-810 is due within 3 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
