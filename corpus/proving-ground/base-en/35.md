# 35 Service card for the МШ-070

This card covers the МШ-070 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The МШ-070 is a laboratory stirrer. The card applies to every build of the МШ-070 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The МШ-070 is verified every 36 months. The МШ-070 is calibrated every 12 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the МШ-070 is the drive belt. It is replaced every 18 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the МШ-070 requires an engineer authorised to level 1. An authorisation below level 1 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The МШ-070 is stored between 0 and 45 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the МШ-070 is due within 2 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
