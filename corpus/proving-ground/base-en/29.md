# 29 Service card for the ДТ-760

This card covers the ДТ-760 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The ДТ-760 is a laboratory detector. The card applies to every build of the ДТ-760 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The ДТ-760 is verified every 12 months. The ДТ-760 is calibrated every 6 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the ДТ-760 is the optical window. It is replaced every 18 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the ДТ-760 requires an engineer authorised to level 3. An authorisation below level 3 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The ДТ-760 is stored between 10 and 32 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the ДТ-760 is due within 5 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
