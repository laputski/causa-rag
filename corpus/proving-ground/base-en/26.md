# 26 Service card for the НС-610

This card covers the НС-610 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The НС-610 is a laboratory pump. The card applies to every build of the НС-610 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The НС-610 is verified every 24 months. The НС-610 is calibrated every 12 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the НС-610 is the shaft seal. It is replaced every 12 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the НС-610 requires an engineer authorised to level 1. An authorisation below level 1 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The НС-610 is stored between -5 and 45 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the НС-610 is due within 2 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
