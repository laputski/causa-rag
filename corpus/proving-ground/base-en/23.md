# 23 Service card for the ТМ-450

This card covers the ТМ-450 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The ТМ-450 is a laboratory thermostat. The card applies to every build of the ТМ-450 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The ТМ-450 is verified every 36 months. The ТМ-450 is calibrated every 6 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the ТМ-450 is the air filter. It is replaced every 3 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the ТМ-450 requires an engineer authorised to level 1. An authorisation below level 1 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The ТМ-450 is stored between 0 and 40 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the ТМ-450 is due within 2 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
