# 25 Service card for the ВС-560

This card covers the ВС-560 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The ВС-560 is a laboratory balance. The card applies to every build of the ВС-560 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The ВС-560 is verified every 12 months. The ВС-560 is calibrated every 2 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the ВС-560 is the calibration weight. It is replaced every 24 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the ВС-560 requires an engineer authorised to level 2. An authorisation below level 2 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The ВС-560 is stored between 15 and 25 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the ВС-560 is due within 3 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
