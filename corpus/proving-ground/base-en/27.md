# 27 Service card for the НС-650

This card covers the НС-650 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The НС-650 is a laboratory pump. The card applies to every build of the НС-650 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The НС-650 is verified every 24 months. The НС-650 is calibrated every 6 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the НС-650 is the shaft seal. It is replaced every 9 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the НС-650 requires an engineer authorised to level 1. An authorisation below level 1 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The НС-650 is stored between -5 and 45 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the НС-650 is due within 2 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
