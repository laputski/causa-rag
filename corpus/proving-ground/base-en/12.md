# 12 Servicing auxiliary equipment

Planned work on pumps and thermostats. Work on the instrument itself is described in section 3 and is not included here.

## What the work covers

Every month the seals are checked for tightness and the pump oil level is read. Every quarter the thermostat air filter is cleaned.

## Downtime

Servicing auxiliary equipment takes no more than two hours and does not require taking the instrument out of service.
