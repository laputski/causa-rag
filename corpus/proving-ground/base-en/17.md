# 17 Service card for the АТ-140

This card covers the АТ-140 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The АТ-140 is a laboratory titrator. The card applies to every build of the АТ-140 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The АТ-140 is verified every 12 months. The АТ-140 is calibrated every 4 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the АТ-140 is the electrode membrane. It is replaced every 9 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the АТ-140 requires an engineer authorised to level 2. An authorisation below level 2 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The АТ-140 is stored between 5 and 35 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the АТ-140 is due within 3 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
