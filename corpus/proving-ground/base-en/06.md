# 6 Taking an instrument out of service

This section describes the procedure by which an instrument stops counting as fit for use.

## Grounds

An instrument is taken out of service on a repeated fault of the same unit within thirty days, or when its verification has expired.

## Marking

An instrument taken out of service carries a red tag with the date and a name. The tag is removed only by the person who attached it, or by the head of service.
