# 2 Verification and calibration

This section separates two pieces of work that are often confused, and gives the interval for each.

## Interval

Verification is performed once every twelve months. Calibration is performed once every three months and after every replacement of a measuring module.

## Who may perform it

Verification of the instrument is performed only by an accredited laboratory. Calibration is performed by a service engineer holding a current authorisation under section 8.
