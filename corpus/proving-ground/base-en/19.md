# 19 Service card for the СФ-260

This card covers the СФ-260 and no other model. The general procedure for every instrument is set out in sections one to fifteen and is not replaced by this card.

## What it is

The СФ-260 is a laboratory spectrophotometer. The card applies to every build of the СФ-260 whatever its year of manufacture.

## Verification and calibration

The СФ-260 is verified every 24 months. The СФ-260 is calibrated every 3 months and after any replacement of the measuring module.

## Consumable parts

The main consumable of the СФ-260 is the deuterium lamp. It is replaced every 18 months, or sooner if self-diagnosis calls for it.

## Personnel authorisation

Servicing the СФ-260 requires an engineer authorised to level 3. An authorisation below level 3 carries the right to external inspection only.

## Storage

The СФ-260 is stored between 10 and 30 degrees. Going outside that range calls for an unscheduled calibration before the instrument is put back to work.

## Reporting

The service report for the СФ-260 is due within 5 working days of the visit. The report carries the serial number and the list of parts replaced.
