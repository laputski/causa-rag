# 1 Receiving an instrument

This section sets out what an engineer does in the first day after an instrument arrives on site, and which records are required.

## Inspecting the packaging

The packaging is opened in the presence of the customer's representative. Damage to the outer case is photographed before opening, or the carrier will not accept a claim.

## The log entry

The receiving log records the serial number, the delivery date and the name of the person who received it. The entry is made on the day of delivery and is never backdated.
