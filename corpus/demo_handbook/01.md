# Purchase approval

This section defines who may approve a purchase at Northwind Robotics and what evidence each approval requires.

## Approval thresholds

Every purchase falls into one of four bands, determined by the total value of the order including tax and shipping.

| Band | Order value | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| A | up to 500 EUR | the requester's team lead |
| B | 500 to 5,000 EUR | the department head |
| C | 5,000 to 50,000 EUR | the finance director |
| D | above 50,000 EUR | the finance director and one board member |

Splitting a single purchase into several smaller orders to stay inside a lower band is treated as a policy violation and is reported under the incident procedure.

## Required evidence

A band A request needs a supplier quote and a one-line justification. Band B adds the expected delivery date and the budget line the cost is drawn from. Band C and band D additionally require two competing quotes from different suppliers, or a written explanation of why only one supplier can serve the request.

Quotes older than 90 days are considered expired. A purchase approved against an expired quote must be re-approved before the order is placed.

## Timing

Approvers have five working days to respond. A request that receives no answer within that window escalates automatically to the next band's approver. Escalation does not change the evidence requirements: a band B request escalated to the finance director is still assessed as a band B request.

Urgent requests may be marked as expedited, which shortens the response window to one working day. The requester must state the operational consequence of waiting. Expedited status may not be applied to band D purchases.

## After approval

The approver's decision is recorded in the purchasing system with the approver's name, the date and the band. Purchase orders are issued only from an approved request, and the order number is written back onto the request so that the two can be reconciled during the monthly close.
