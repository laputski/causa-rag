# Receiving equipment

This section describes what happens between a delivery arriving at the loading bay and the equipment becoming available for use.

## Accepting a delivery

Deliveries are accepted at the loading bay between 08:00 and 16:00 on working days. The receiving clerk checks the delivery note against the purchase order number before signing.

A delivery whose order number does not match any open purchase order is refused. A delivery with visible external damage is accepted with the damage noted on the carrier's paperwork and photographed before the packaging is opened.

Partial deliveries are accepted. The delivery note is marked with the quantity actually received, and the purchase order stays open until the remainder arrives or the supplier confirms cancellation.

## Inspection

Every item is inspected within two working days of arrival. The inspection confirms the model and serial number, checks for transit damage, and verifies that the documentation the purchase order required is present.

Equipment that fails inspection is quarantined in the marked area of the parts store and is not entered into the asset register. The supplier is notified within one working day of the failed inspection.

## Asset registration

Equipment passing inspection is entered into the asset register with its serial number, purchase order number, acquisition date, cost and assigned owner. Items costing 1,000 EUR or more additionally receive a printed asset tag fixed to the housing.

The assigned owner is a named person rather than a team. Ownership transfers are recorded in the register, and the register is reconciled against the physical inventory during the annual stock count.

## Calibration

Measuring equipment is calibrated before first use, regardless of any calibration certificate supplied by the vendor. The calibration date and the next due date are recorded against the asset.

Equipment past its calibration due date is withdrawn from use until recalibrated. The parts store checks due dates weekly and notifies the owner four weeks before expiry.
