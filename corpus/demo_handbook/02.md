# Travel and expenses

This section covers business travel booking, daily allowances and the deadlines for submitting expense claims.

## Booking

Travel is booked through the company travel desk. Employees who book directly are reimbursed at the lower of the actual cost and the fare the travel desk could have obtained on the same day.

Flights under four hours are booked in economy class. Flights of four hours or longer may be booked in premium economy. Business class requires the department head's written approval and is granted only for flights longer than eight hours.

Rail travel is preferred over flying where the total door-to-door journey time is within one hour of the equivalent flight.

## Daily allowance

The daily allowance covers meals and incidental costs. It is paid per full day away from the usual place of work.

| Destination | Daily allowance |
|---|---|
| Domestic | 45 EUR |
| Within Europe | 65 EUR |
| Outside Europe | 85 EUR |

Departure and return days are paid at half the daily rate. Where a meal is provided by a conference or a host, the allowance for that day is reduced by one third per provided meal.

The daily allowance requires no receipts. Costs claimed on top of the allowance are reimbursed only against an itemised receipt.

## Accommodation

Accommodation is reimbursed at actual cost up to 180 EUR per night in major cities and 120 EUR per night elsewhere. Exceeding the cap requires the department head's approval, given before the booking rather than after.

## Submitting a claim

Expense claims are submitted within 30 calendar days of returning. A claim submitted after 60 days is paid only with the finance director's approval, and the reason for the delay is recorded.

Each claim carries the trip's purpose, the dates, and a receipt for every line except the daily allowance. Claims are paid with the payroll run that follows their approval.
