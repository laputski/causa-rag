# Site access and contractors

This section states who may enter Northwind Robotics premises, how visitors and contractors are admitted, and what happens when a badge is lost.

## Badges

Every employee carries a photo badge. The badge opens the doors matching the holder's access class and is worn visibly inside the building.

Three access classes exist. Class 1 covers offices and shared areas. Class 2 adds the assembly floor. Class 3 adds the calibration room and the parts store. Access class is assigned by the site manager on the request of the department head, and is reviewed every six months.

A lost badge is reported to the site manager the same day. The lost badge is deactivated within one hour of the report, and a replacement is issued the next working day. A second replacement within twelve months carries a 25 EUR fee.

## Visitors

Visitors are registered before arrival with a name, a company and the employee hosting them. On arrival they sign in at reception and receive a visitor badge valid for that day only.

Visitors are escorted at all times in class 2 and class 3 areas. An escorted visitor group may not exceed six people per escort. Visitors do not operate equipment under any circumstances.

## Contractors

Contractors working on site for more than five consecutive days receive a temporary badge instead of a daily visitor badge. The temporary badge expires on the contract end date recorded at issue, and extending it requires a new request rather than an adjustment.

Before a temporary badge is issued, the contractor provides proof of liability insurance and completes the site safety briefing. The briefing is repeated annually.

Contractors requiring class 3 access are additionally approved by the head of engineering, and their work in the calibration room is supervised by a Northwind employee.

## Out-of-hours access

The building is open from 07:00 to 20:00 on working days. Entry outside those hours is logged separately and requires the site manager to be notified in advance, by message or email, stating the expected departure time.
